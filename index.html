Theoretical vs. experimental probability

Set a theoretical distribution to compare your prediction with the results from the physical board. These percentages are an example model, not a guarantee: real outcomes depend on the board design and ball behavior.

Theoretical percentages should add up to 100%.

SlotTheoreticalExperimentalDifference (percentage points)

Formula

Experimental probability = (observed frequency ÷ total trials) × 100%

Difference = experimental probability − theoretical probability

A positive difference means the outcome occurred more often than the model predicted; a negative difference means it occurred less often.

What this project demonstrates

📐 Mathematics

Probability, possible paths, combinations, and Pascal’s triangle for an idealized left/right movement model.

📊 Statistics

Frequency distributions, experimental probability, mean, median, mode, range, and visualizing collected results.

💻 BSCS integration

The app processes user input, stores observations during the session, automates calculations, and exports a CSV file for further analysis.

Presentation tip: Explain that the app records the physical experiment; it does not predict the exact path of each real ball. Compare repeated observations with your stated theoretical model.
